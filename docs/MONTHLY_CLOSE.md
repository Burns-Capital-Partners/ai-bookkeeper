# Monthly close checklist

Goal: the previous calendar month's books are ready by the following 7th. Daily processing is a means to that goal; daily displayed balances need not agree across different cutoffs.

Read current private status, context and accepted source evidence. Reuse completed checks with valid coverage. This checklist covers agent/operator work; it is broader than the current automated worker checklist.

## 1. Assemble the evidence

- [ ] Confirm entity, month, report basis, source coverage and runtime revision.
- [ ] Verify bank and card originals, including activity between billing dates and calendar month-end; retain opening/closing balances and source IDs.
- [ ] Obtain mortgage principal/interest/escrow, escrow disbursements and other in-scope loan evidence.
- [ ] Tie PM/wallet gross rent, fees, refunds and settlement routes to leases/receipts. Keep late/unpaid rent visible without inventing receipts.
- [ ] Check tenant deposits, custody, refunds/carryovers and interest separately where in scope; exclude refundable balances from operating/spendable cash.
- [ ] Attach receipts and capital invoices to their existing transactions. Record missing evidence and purpose questions in one batch.

## 2. Apply authorized work and reconcile

- [ ] Review existing Zoho entries and prior corrections; only post missing approved effects. Inspect backlog scope before invoking `categorize`.
- [ ] Verify returned Zoho IDs, actual amounts, date, publication and report-basis visibility after posting.
- [ ] Bridge bank/card/escrow/loan balances to the same cutoff, recording every reconciling item. Compare current balances too when an opening correction is involved.
- [ ] Check expense versus asset, principal versus interest, deposits versus rent, personal versus property spending and owner/intercompany transfers.
- [ ] Match both sides of transfers/payments once. Follow any other-entity charge through its reimbursement/handoff; do not strand an unexplained clearing balance.
- [ ] Complete and retain native Zoho reconciliation evidence where required; worker audit results are separate evidence.
- [ ] Rerun unchanged inputs for the first accepted month and after changes affecting posting/matching; prove no additional ledger entries or balance movement.

## 3. Publish a concise close result

- [ ] Save P&L, balance sheet and relevant account/loan/deposit schedules on the confirmed basis, with dates/filters and links to reconciliation.
- [ ] Show material exceptions and provisional assumptions with amount, proposed treatment, owner and next action. State excluded accounts/periods explicitly.
- [ ] Record closing status accurately: reconciled, reconciled with specified exceptions, or incomplete. Unexplained material differences cannot be waived into "reconciled."
- [ ] Publish worker/cloud evidence and verify the actual notification receipt. Update the same private status and stop repetitive reminders when resolved.
- [ ] Surface a few optional saving opportunities separately; distinguish profit, operating cash and funds owed to tenants.
- [ ] Complete the [improvement loop](CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT.md): refresh the canonical [entity record](ENTITY_RECORD.md), verify shared readback, and put any newly demonstrated reusable fix into the actual checklist/code with appropriate tests. Record repo publication separately from deployment adoption.

An expected late payment can remain a collection follow-up under the approved policy. A balance discrepancy, missing source period or duplicate risk requires separate resolution and must remain visible.
